On Home, Settings, the Recycle Bin and What's new, Ctrl+K, Ctrl+F, Ctrl+Shift+F, Ctrl+L, Alt+Up and Alt+Left/Right work: the page steps aside (a tab opened just for it closes) and the key acts on the folder. (#77)
