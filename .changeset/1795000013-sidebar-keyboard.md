Screen readers and the keyboard can reach the sidebar: F6 (Shift+F6 back) moves between the file list, the sidebar, the path bar and the search box; in the sidebar the arrow keys move between rows, Right and Left expand and collapse, Enter opens. Screen readers can open and expand sidebar rows even when they are scrolled out of sight (#322)
