Favorite tiles move like app icons on a phone: a dragged tile lifts under the pointer, the others slide aside to make room, and it settles into its new place when you let go. While you drag a favorite, the Settings row at the bottom of the sidebar turns into a "Remove from favorites" target; dropping it there takes it off the favorites.
