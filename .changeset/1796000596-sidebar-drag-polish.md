Sidebar favorites: a folder dragged from the list up to the tiles makes room among them right away; dropping on a long-named favorite puts it in the list; a folder dragged over the tiles shows one sign of where it goes; and clicking a favorite no longer scrolls the sidebar down to the folder tree.
