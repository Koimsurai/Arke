Ctrl+click a folder in the sidebar, on Home or in the address bar to open it in a new tab in the background, or Ctrl+Shift+click to switch to it; in the file list, where Ctrl+click selects, Ctrl+double-click does the same. Middle-click also works in the address bar now. (#291)
