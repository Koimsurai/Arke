Zip and 7z files made by 7-Zip, WinRAR, Bandizip and others extract even when they use a compression method other than the usual one: Deflate64, BZip2, LZMA, XZ, zstd and PPMd in zip, BZip2, Deflate and zstd in 7z. One compressed in a way Arke still can't read offers to open it with your default app (#341)
