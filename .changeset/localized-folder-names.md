Folders named by their desktop.ini, such as Desktop, Documents and Downloads in your user folder on a non-English Windows, show that name as in File Explorer, also when an older program wrote the desktop.ini in your language's legacy encoding: the list sorts, filters and jumps by it, and renaming one changes that name while the folder keeps its own (#370)
