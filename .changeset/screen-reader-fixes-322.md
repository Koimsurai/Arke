Screen readers can use more of Arke: each version on the What's new page says whether it is expanded and its notes can be read, the setup guide takes the focus when it opens (Tab stays in it, Esc closes it and the focus goes back), and the Sort and View options menus open from the keyboard on the checked option, with the arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to pick and Esc to close (#322)
